00. BODYWEIGHT SQUAT
====================================================================================
[confident] Everyone squats. Almost nobody squats right.

Your legs are three engines... the glutes, the hamstrings, and the quads on the front of the thigh.

Arms forward. Sit your hips back, chest proud, knees pushing out over your toes. Go deep with your heels down... then drive up through the whole foot. Three sets of fifteen.

01. FALSE-GRIP HANG
====================================================================================
[confident] Big arms... but small forearms? Here's the fix.

Your forearm has three muscle groups... the extensors on top, the flexors underneath, and the brachioradialis by the elbow.

Put the heels of your hands on top of the bar, wrists bent over it. Hang with straight arms and keep those wrists curled. Three hangs of twenty to thirty seconds.
