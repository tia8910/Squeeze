SQUEEZE FIT: GROK PROMPTS FOR 30-SECOND SHORTS
Three clips per exercise, joined into one 30-second Short:

  Clip A  HOOK      0–3 s    close-up of the body part, a glowing measuring ring
  Clip B  ANATOMY   3–10 s   the limb on its own, muscles lighting up one by one in
                             blue, green and red (I add the coloured name labels)
  Clip C  DEMO      10–27 s  the exercise, the working muscle glowing red
          END CARD  27–30 s  added by me

How to use:
- Image-to-video in Grok, highest quality. Start every clip from start-frame.png (the
  sculpture in the Squeeze Fit studio). For Clip B you can also start from a plain
  navy image; the studio does not need to show.
- Paste the whole prompt for each clip. Generate 2 versions of Clip C and keep the best.
- No text in any clip: every label and title is added by me, in the brand style.
- Name the files by exercise and letter, e.g. 01A.mp4, 01B.mp4, 01C.mp4.
- The colours matter: keep blue, green and red in the order given, because the labels
  and the voiceover follow that order.
