## Loops to generate (image to video)

Fixed camera, same figure, same dark navy background, seamless loop, no text changes, 720p or better.

1. `coach_turn.mp4` from coach_front: turns slowly front, quarter, side, back, and back to front. About 6 s.
2. `coach_vacuum.mp4` from coach_quarter: side on, breathes out fully, pulls the belly in under the ribs, holds, releases. About 5 s.
3. `coach_squat.mp4` from coach_quarter: side on, one slow bodyweight squat to just below parallel, chest up. About 4 s.
4. `coach_pushup.mp4` from coach_quarter: side on, in a plank, one slow push-up with a straight body line. About 4 s.
5. `coach_curl.mp4` from coach_front: front on, one slow bicep curl with both arms, elbows by the sides. About 4 s.
