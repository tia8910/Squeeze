## Full description

Squeeze Fit reads your body fat from a single photo, then builds your training week and your meals around it. Everything happens on your phone. Your photos are never uploaded, not to us and not to anyone.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Most body scan apps send your photos to a server. Squeeze Fit does not have one.
• Every scan is analysed on your phone, even in airplane mode
• Photos never leave your device
• No account needed, nothing to sign up for
• All your data is stored encrypted and locked with your fingerprint or face
• No ads, no analytics, no trackers, no data sold or shared
• Backup is optional and goes only to a hidden folder in your own Google Drive

BODY SCAN FROM ONE PHOTO
• Body fat percentage and lean mass from a front photo and your weight
• A trend line that shows real change and filters out day to day noise
• Covered areas are left out, so shorts never count against your legs
• Your full scan history, kept on your phone

PHYSIQUE ANALYSIS
• Every muscle group read from your scan: shoulders, arms, chest, back, abs and legs
• See what is strong and what to bring up next, matched to your goal
• Your weak points feed straight into your training plan

TRAINING BUILT FOR YOU
• Choose your sports: gym, calisthenics, running, Pilates, yoga and more, or combine them
• A weekly plan built for your goal, your experience and the time you train
• Log every set and get progressive overload advice for the next one
• Photograph a gym machine to see how to use it and which exercises to do on it

NUTRITION THAT FITS YOUR LIFE
• Calories and macros calculated from your body and your goal
• Meals built from foods you actually like
• Meal times planned around your training
• 11 micronutrients tracked so nothing runs short

DAILY COACHING
• Tips for your goal and your training time, every day
• Clear next steps after every scan

Built for adults who want honest numbers without giving up their privacy.

Squeeze Fit is a fitness and nutrition tool, not a medical device. Talk to a doctor before starting a new diet or training plan.

Privacy policy: https://squeeze.fit/privacy
